## Notes

- **The "new" Trivy findings on `nginx` are not new vulnerabilities.** They are the same three Debian records
  (CVE-2009-4487, CVE-2013-0337, CVE-2023-44487) that Grype matches by package name in *both* images. On the
  baseline, Trivy 0.74.0 skipped the nginx.org package (maintainer `NGINX Packaging`); our package has a
  different maintainer and version string, and Trivy now matches it against Debian's `nginx` source package.
- **Neither scanner checks nginx itself against nginx.org's advisories.** CVE-2024-7347 is not reported on either
  image, so its fix cannot show up here. The evidence is the build (`build/patches/`) and the compatibility test
  check `cve-2024-7347`, recorded in `vex/CVE-2024-7347.openvex.json`. A binary-level scan that does report it, and
  the VEX suppressing it on our image only, is in `vex-demo.md` (`make vex`). Residual nginx CVEs: `TRIAGE.md`.
- **The removed modules are counted separately on purpose.** They are an extra hardening step (smaller attack
  surface), not one of the selected CVE fixes.
